# Proofwork Companion Course Contract

## Course Identity

**Working title:** Build a Copilot Interview Practice Lab

**Audience:** Job seekers who want repeatable, role-specific interview practice after tailoring a resume or building a career evidence bank.

**Creator and instructor:** Dr. Vicki Bealman

**Duration:** 60 minutes across four working lessons.

**Course promise:** Build a reusable Microsoft Copilot interview-preparation agent that converts a real job posting and verified career evidence into an interview brief, a defensible story bank, an adaptive mock interview, and a consistent evidence-led debrief. The agent must never invent candidate experience or coach the learner to hide a genuine gap.

**Student artifacts:** Editable notes, the exact lesson prompt, nonconfidential completion evidence, and an applied reflection. Each lesson produces a Canvas-ready UTF-8 text file; the course also produces a combined bundle and a completion certificate at 100%.

## Four-Lesson Arc

| Lesson | Working title | Artifact created |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Build the interview brief | Reusable agent shell, role analysis, and interview target map |
| 02 | Turn evidence into proof stories | Verified story bank with role, action, result, and follow-up evidence |
| 03 | Run the room, one question at a time | Adaptive mock-interview protocol with realistic probes and no premature coaching |
| 04 | Debrief what changed | Fixed evidence rubric, transcript review, gap report, and repeatable practice plan |

## Lesson Contract

Every lesson will include one measurable objective, a three-paragraph editorial reading, exactly three memorable moves, one reusable Copilot prompt with explicit truth rules, one action assignment, one applied reflection, and one Canvas deliverable.

## Companion-Site Contract

The course will live at `/interview-prep` inside the existing Proofwork project. It will preserve the parent brand and Dr. Bealman attribution while using a distinct interview-room composition, dedicated course data, independent browser storage, separate filenames, and original visual assets. The current resume course will remain available at `/`.

## Truth and Privacy Boundaries

The agent must distinguish verified evidence, transferable evidence, partial evidence, and gaps. It must not infer credentials, ownership, dates, tools, outcomes, employer details, protected characteristics, or sensitive personal information. Students will be reminded to remove confidential employer or client material before using Copilot or uploading files to Canvas.
